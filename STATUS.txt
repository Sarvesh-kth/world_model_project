WHAT IS LEARNED AND WHAT IS A RULE (2026-10-12 evening, the state the 100-scene run was made with)
=======================================================================================================
Learned: V-JEPA 2 (frozen) -> z; Q (cube position from the static grid, held from wrist + gripper); D (latent
and robot state 8 steps ahead, robot head reads Q); R (penalties: proximity, contact, table hit, empty grasp,
weight 1). The planner is an MPC: waypoint + gripper-switch candidates, a go-to rule, D imagines, Q and R read,
CEM; the SCORE is hand-written (stage potential reach 0-.5 / held 1-2.5 / placed 3.5, linear pulls .1 to the
cube and .8 to the goal, penalties).
Rules on top of the models (each with its measured reason, controller/README.md "The planner"):
  1 gripper envelope: an imagined hold counts only if the imagined hand was within 1.5 cm xy / 2 cm z of Q's
    cube when the fingers closed (D imagines holds for closes mid-travel far from the cube, absent from data)
  2 width rules: fingers under 2 cm hold nothing; fingers commanded shut at 3.5-6.5 cm hold something (Q's
    held lags a real grasp ~12 steps, 16-frame time-mean latent); real and imagined states
  3 release only within .5 radius of the goal while held, else go to the goal low and open there; imagined
    placed needs .4 radius (Q reads a just-released cube 2-4 cm toward the hand)
  4 geometry constants: grasp site >= 2.2 cm above the table, descend when aligned within 2 cm, switch within
    2 cm, hand still on the closing step, carry 8 cm, commitment margin .05, imagined width in [-2, 12] cm
  5 fingers never turn (fine for the cube only)
Obstacles are NOT avoided by the models: planner.ObstacleMap (depth height map, hand disc and cube masked,
readings >= 20 cm discarded, 3 agreeing readings) gives a clearance penalty on the imagined hand and an A*
route whose next point is the travel proposal; R's proximity is added at weight 1 but is not what keeps the
hand out of walls. The all-model alternatives (new, 2026-10-12 evening): --obstacle-source model = the
obstacle model of world_model/train_obstacle.py (a 5x5 window of the height map around the hand, the hand's
height and in-cell offset, the gripper numbers -> proximity, P(contact); on the IMAGINED latent and robot
state) instead of the map and the route; --no-goal = the planner is not told the goal, R's terms read off the
image (the green place marker is in the frame) carry the task, no goal proposals / pull / release rule.
Obstacle model validation: proximity 0.32 off when penalised (the 8 cm cells cannot resolve the 6 cm proximity
ramp, a limit of the sensor resolution, same as R), contact AUC 0.81; a convolution over the whole map
memorised scenes (best epoch 1), the hand window did not.

REVIEW (2026-10-12 early morning): why 1,300 episodes were not enough, and the v3 chain
==========================================================================================
Question: a world model should not need 1,300 episodes for one cube; it still grasps badly and goes to the
wrong side. Measured on the data/episodes_v2 latents (76k encoded states, scratch scripts q_probe / res_probe):
  1. Q's error grows with the hand's distance from the cube: xy MAE 0.3/0.3 cm under 3 cm, 1.2/1.4 at 8-20 cm,
     2.7/4.4 cm beyond 20 cm (val). Far = a clean, unoccluded view, the moment the planner picks its direction.
     Retrained without any robot state the far error stayed at 3.3/4.2 cm: not a shortcut, the 8x8 x 16-PCA
     time-mean latent itself cannot place a 15 px cube. On 4,000 sampled frames (2,863 train, no robot state):
        MLP on 8x8 PCA16 (what was used)        far-hand xy MAE 3.3 / 6.4 cm
        conv + spatial softmax, 16x16 PCA16     1.4 / 1.4
        conv + spatial softmax, 16x16 PCA32     0.8 / 0.8
        conv + spatial softmax, 16x16 PCA64     0.7 / 0.8
     That is the data-efficiency problem: the position has to be READ OFF the grid, not fitted by an MLP.
  2. Q, R, D all memorised: best validation epoch 3 (R), 13 (Q), training loss 7-10x below validation; D's
     validation latent MSE 0.16 against 0.27 for simply keeping z constant. Consecutive states of a scene are
     near duplicates, so 150k states were ~1,000 independent scenes.
  3. Bias: every episodes_v2 episode has the cube in the four end zones (never the middle band); full_v2,
     obstacles_v2 and planner_v1 (46k of the 123k training states) are at ONE spot, and the default empty
     benchmark (no --random-ends) is that spot, so "empty 10/10" was a memorised scene. The honest number was
     the random-end obstacle run, 4/10. Last night's planner constants were tuned on one 3-obstacle scene and
     took the empty run from 10/10 to 2/7.
  Already fine: the proximity penalty is the closest distance over all 24 arm collision geoms; the cube spawns
  at a random yaw every episode and the scripted kinds turn the fingers to its faces.
Changes (uncommitted, on top of the previous session's anywhere-sampler, wander and lateral kinds):
  encode.py   per camera grid and PCA: static 16x16 x 32 (--static-pool 1 --static-pca 32), wrist 8x8 x 16;
              z = 8192 + 1024 + 64 = 9280; meta.json has spatial_pool / cells / pca_dims per camera.
  train.py    Q = GridReadout by default (1x1 + two 3x3 convolutions per camera, spatial softmax over the
              static grid), input = z + finger width + gripper command only (P_GRIPPER), two position heads
